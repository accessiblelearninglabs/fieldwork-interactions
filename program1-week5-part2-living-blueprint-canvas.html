Field 1

What are you asking, and for whom? State the question, scoped to what you can carry.

Show example
Example · composite How do adult learners who rely on screen readers experience our onboarding course, and where does it drop them?

Field 2

How will you gather evidence and make sense of it? Could someone else follow these steps?

Show example
Example · composite Three accessibility walkthroughs of the course with screen-reader users, recorded with consent. Barriers coded against WCAG and against what users say actually stopped them.

Field 3

What changes, and for whom? Does this name a change in someone's experience, not only a gap in the literature?

Show example
Example · composite A named list of barriers the team can fix before the next cohort, so the learners currently dropped are no longer designed out.

Field 4

How would any reader judge whether it worked? Is success defined by the most excluded learner, or by what would satisfy you?

Show example
Example · composite At least the top three barriers removed and re-tested with the same users, who confirm the course now reaches them.

Field 5

The commitment that must not change. Which commitment, if broken, would mean the inquiry lost the person it was for?

Show example
Example · composite The screen-reader users judge whether it works. Not the analytics, and not the team.

Field 6

The parts you expect the work to correct. Where do you already anticipate reality editing the plan?

Show example
Example · composite The method. Three walkthroughs may be too few, and the coding scheme will likely change once real barriers appear.
