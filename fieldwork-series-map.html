Fieldwork — the series map

Fieldwork is a research method for inclusive learning design, practised in public. It asks one question: in education, at work, in public service, how does learning design decide who is included? Twenty-four posts in four parts. Each one reads a single adjudicated decision from the public record through the same instrument, adds one finding to the tally, and hands the reader one entry to make from a setting of their own. Open any post to see what it reads, what entry it adds to the audit, and what can be done afterwards.

The question the series carries from the first post to the last: when a learning or working system was found to have failed someone, which of the three conditions could not act, and did anything in the outcome require the system to learn?

Three limits, stated here and returned to in Post 24. Adjudicated cases are the failures that were fought, so the corpus cannot see the ones that never reached a tribunal. Reading a judgment is not reading an institution. The series reads for learning design; it is not legal advice.
