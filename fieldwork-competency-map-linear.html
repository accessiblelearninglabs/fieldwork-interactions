Fieldwork Toolkit — the competency map

Linear, accessible version. Ten competencies in three groups, and the relationships between them.

How to read this

This is the plain-text, screen-reader and keyboard-friendly version of the interactive competency map. Nothing is hidden and nothing needs to be opened: every competency appears in full, in order, with its group and its relationships written out. Use your screen reader’s heading navigation to move between groups (heading level 2) and competencies (heading level 3).

On the interactive map, position and colour carry the grouping and the lines carry the relationships. Here both are stated in words, so nothing depends on seeing the diagram.
