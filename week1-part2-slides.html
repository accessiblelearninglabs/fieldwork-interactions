What a statement of intent is not
A statement of intent
is not a thesis.
Not a research question. Not a plan. Not a prediction.
Thesis · Hypothesis · Plan · Prediction
It is something smaller and more honest: a public declaration of what you are paying attention to, and why — before you know what it means.

Worked example — what it looks like together
A statement in three parts.
"I am investigating the gap between the accessible formats policy in our onboarding program and the actual experience of new employees who request alternatives.
Phenomenon
As a designer inside this system, I have shaped some of these materials myself — which means I have both useful access and real blank spots.
Point of view
This inquiry matters now because our department is about to scale the program to three new sites, and whatever is broken in the current design will be replicated at scale."

What makes it a statement of intent
Make it visible.
Kept private
Useful for your own thinking. Stays yours.
Made public
Becomes accountable. Invites others in. Changes through contact.
In inclusive design, that willingness is not just good research practice. It is an ethical position.
